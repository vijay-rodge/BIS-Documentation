## Detailed Scenario Matrix

| ID | Scenario Title | Input Query | Target Outcome & Success Criteria |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **S1** | Standard Discovery | *"Which standard applies to motorcycle helmets?"* | Resolves `IS 4151:2020`, High Confidence, BSB Edge preview link. |
| **S2** | Contextual Follow-up | *"What are the key safety requirements for it?"* | Resolves pronoun 'it' to IS 4151, extracts impact & mass rules. |
| **S3** | Mandatory QCO Check | *"Is BIS certification mandatory for helmets?"* | Confirms `MANDATORY`, cites MoRTH QCO order and gazette date. |
| **S4** | Testing Requirements | *"What laboratory tests are required for helmets?"* | Lists impact attenuation, chin strap, retention tests under Clause 5. |
| **S5** | Clause-Level Inquiry | *"What does clause 5.2 of IS 4151 specify?"* | Extracts Clause 5.2 verbatim: maximum mass less than or equal to 1.5 kg (1500 g). |
| **S6** | Laboratory Search | *"Which laboratories can test two-wheeler helmets?"* | Returns ICAT Manesar, ARAI Pune, and BIS Central Laboratory. |
| **S7** | Multilingual Query | *"मोटरसाइकिल हेलमेट के लिए कौन सा मानक लागू होता है?"* | Responds in fluent Hindi citing `IS 4151:2020` and ISI mark rules. |
| **S8** | Zero-Hallucination | *"What BIS standard applies to interstellar passenger warp drive craft?"* | Strict safe refusal: Declares insufficient evidence, zero hallucination. |
