# Mandatory Certification & QCO Rules

Under Indian law, mandatory certification is established when a line ministry publishes a Quality Control Order in the Gazette of India under Section 16 of the BIS Act, 2016.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    Order[Line Ministry: e.g. Ministry of Steel] --> S_O[Gazette Notification: S.O. 1234(E)]
    S_O --> EnforceDate[Enforcement Date: e.g. 2024-06-01]
    EnforceDate --> CheckMSME{MSME Tier?}
    CheckMSME -->|Large Enterprise| Date1[Standard Effective Date]
    CheckMSME -->|Small Enterprise| Date2[+6 Months Extended Window]
    CheckMSME -->|Micro Enterprise| Date3[+12 Months Extended Window]
```

---

## Categories of Certification Mandate

| Status Label | Statutory Meaning | Legal Consequence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`MANDATORY`** | Enforced under active Gazette QCO | Illegal to manufacture, import, store, or sell without ISI mark |
| **`MANDATORY (DEFERRED)`**| Gazetted but effective date is in the future | Manufacturers must prepare and obtain license before deadline |
| **`VOLUNTARY`** | Published IS standard under Scheme-I | Voluntary quality differentiator; not legally compulsory |
| **`CRS MANDATORY`** | Electronics under Scheme-II (MeitY) | Self-declaration registration required on crsbis.in |
