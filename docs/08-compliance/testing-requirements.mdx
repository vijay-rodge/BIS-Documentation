# Testing Protocols & Compliance Limits

The `TestingAgent` extracts statutory testing requirements defined in standard specifications to provide manufacturers and laboratories with clear acceptance criteria.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    Clause[Testing Clause in IS Document] --> Method[1. Standard Test Method Reference]
    Clause --> Sample[2. Sampling & Specimen Preparation]
    Clause --> Parameter[3. Quantitative Metric & Units]
    Clause --> Limit[4. Mandatory Pass/Fail Threshold]
```

---

## Example: IS 4151:2020 Testing Schedule

- **Clause 5.2 — Mass**: Protective helmet mass must not exceed **1.5 kg (1500 g)**.
- **Clause 5.3 — Impact Attenuation**: Headform acceleration shall not exceed **300 g** for any duration, and **150 g** for longer than 5 ms.
- **Clause 5.4 — Retention System**: Dynamic displacement under load must not exceed 35 mm, with residual displacement less than or equal to 25 mm.
