# QCO Validation & Gazette Citation

Every declaration of mandatory certification must be validated against official Gazette of India records.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    QCO_DB[(QCO Database)] --> Verify[Verification Engine]
    Verify --> CheckMin[Validate Issuing Ministry]
    Verify --> CheckSO[Validate S.O. Number]
    Verify --> CheckExempt[Validate Export Exemptions]
    Verify --> Output[Attach Gazette Citation Badge]
```
