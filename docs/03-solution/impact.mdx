# National Economic & Industrial Impact

Standardization is the bedrock of industrial scalability, consumer safety, and international export competitiveness.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Accessible Standards Information] --> B[Widespread MSME Adoption]
    B --> C[Higher Product Quality & Safety]
    C --> D[Reduced Import of Substandard Goods]
    D --> E[Global Export Competitiveness]
    E --> F[Strengthened 'Make in India' Brand]
```

---

## 1. Advancing "Make in India" & "Atmanirbhar Bharat"
- **Domestic Quality Elevation**: By simplifying access to Quality Control Orders, the platform ensures domestic manufacturers produce goods that compete with global imports in durability, safety, and energy efficiency.
- **Import Curbing**: Facilitates enforcement of mandatory QCOs on steel, chemicals, electrical equipment, and toys, preventing the dumping of cheap, substandard foreign goods into Indian markets.

---

## 2. MSME Empowerment & Ease of Doing Business
- India's 63 million MSMEs form the backbone of the industrial economy.
- Removing regulatory friction directly supports the Government of India's **Ease of Doing Business (EoDB)** index by providing a transparent, self-serve compliance portal.

---

## 3. Consumer Welfare & Public Health Protection
- Ensures that critical safety items—such as motorcycle helmets, domestic pressure cookers, and infant feeding bottles—are understood as non-negotiable safety items covered by criminal law.
- Empowers consumers to identify fake ISI marks and file complaints directly on the BIS Care portal.
